30 m source cells are not individual trees.

Forest, wetland, crops and grass remain separate classes. Imagery is resampled for display; vegetation height, tree spacing, understory and seasonal conditions remain unknown.

Inventory positions have limited precision.

A point is the source record location, not an exact bridge outline. Its relationship to the mapped shoreline is unverified.

Water conditions remain separate.

Geometry does not establish seasonal depth, velocity, flooding, ice, potability or crossing suitability.

Buildings and roads are excluded from the current scope. These environmental layers do not assess passage or route availability.

Source resolution remains 30 m. USGS land-cover imagery is reprojected and resampled for the web view; finer display pixels and zoom do not add information. Local class queries are separate requests with approximately 30 m ground support.

Independent external services. USGS land-cover images and Overture water geometry load separately. Failures stay visible and do not remove available layers. Water strokes are cartographic lines, not measurements of channel width.

Independent dates. Land cover and bridge inventory describe dated snapshots. Gauge records and climate describe the selected calendar month across 2006–2025. None is a forecast of a particular day.

The climate baseline

How to read the map

  • Precipitation is the average of 20 totals for the selected calendar month, expressed as liquid water equivalent.
  • Temperature is a monthly average. The daily-high and daily-low layers average daily extrema; they are not the most extreme events.
  • No-data cells are unknown, not dry, easy or safe.
  • Elevation is a separately dated landscape snapshot. It is not a monthly climatology or a local slope measurement.
  • Land cover distinguishes forest, shrubland, grassland, pasture, crops, wetlands and open water. The underlying source has 30 m cells; this coarse service overview has roughly 4–7 km spacing. Native-pixel correspondence, local majority class and fine boundaries are unverified.
  • Bridge records appear as individual points in Land & water detail. A reported location or feature name does not establish crossing suitability.

INTERPRETATION PRINCIPLES

Keep the evidence and its limits visible.

This atlas shows public environmental and infrastructure source records. Its colors are physical measurements or land-cover categories, and it does not calculate a combined vehicle score.

Preserve source meaning

Categories retain their labels. Climate values retain units and a separate 20-year baseline for each calendar month.

Respect scale and date

A coarse service class, a water outline and a bridge inventory point have different spatial support and observation dates.

Retain unknowns

Missing layers and load failures remain visible. Crew comfort and drinking-water supply stay separate from environmental water.

ⓘ

This is a public environmental atlas. The mapped measurements, classes and infrastructure records do not establish vehicle passage, a usable crossing or a safe drinking-water source.
